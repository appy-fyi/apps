Steady Gallery — Play Console "App access" declaration
========================================================

Package id to use: fyi.appy.steadygridgallery (renamed 2026-08-21 from

Q: Is any part of your app restricted?
A: Yes

Reason: no accounts/sign-in exist, but two features gate content behind a
local PIN/biometric prompt or a purchase, so reviewers need instructions
to reach them. Add TWO separate instruction entries (Play Console lets you
add multiple named entries) — leave Username/Password blank on both, since
there is no login of any kind.

ENTRY 1
Name/label field:
  Hidden Photos (PIN / biometric lock)

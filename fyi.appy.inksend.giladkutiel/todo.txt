    Image Generation Pipeline:
        We try to understand the intent of the text, possible intents (10 in total) are exactly:
            FUNNY, SAD, ROMANTIC, ANGRY, INFORMATIVE, EXCITED, CELEBRATORY, CALM, MOTIVATIONAL, GRATEFUL

        Each intent is mapped to a list of least 2 font families (2 for English, 2 for Hebrew)
        Each intent is mapped to at least 4 color gradients (common to all languages).
        In addition, we have a predefined list of at least 100 popular diverse emojis, and a dictionary that maps stems to emojis (e.g. "fun" -> 😂, "sad" -> 😢, "romanc" -> ❤️, etc...).
        If we cannot detect the intent of the text we fall back to sentiment analysis (VADER rules).
        Sentiment is mapped to (multiple) intents.
        Emojis are always selected based on the keyword lookup, if no keywords are found we use a default emoji (🫟).
